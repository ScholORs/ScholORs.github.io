Pioneering Operations Research for a Smarter World

ScholORs Lab is committed to pushing the boundaries of operations research. Our interdisciplinary team combines mathematical rigor with computational innovation to develop algorithms and models that tackle complex optimization problems across industry, logistics, healthcare, and beyond.
