Focus Areas

Core Research Directions

Combinatorial Optimization

We develop exact algorithms, metaheuristics, and matheuristics for large-scale combinatorial problems. Our work addresses classic challenges such as the Traveling Salesman Problem, Vehicle Routing, and Graph Coloring, while extending these frameworks to novel industrial applications.

Our approach combines integer programming, constraint programming, and hybrid methods to push the boundaries of solvable problem sizes.

Related Publications →

Machine Learning for OR

We leverage deep reinforcement learning, graph neural networks, and attention-based architectures to learn optimization policies, predict branching decisions, and accelerate mathematical programming solvers.

Our goal is to create self-improving optimization systems that generalize across problem instances and domains.

Related Publications →

Supply Chain Analytics

We model complex, multi-echelon supply networks under uncertainty, incorporating sustainability metrics, disruption resilience, and demand variability into our optimization frameworks.

Our solutions help organizations design robust, efficient, and environmentally conscious supply chains.

Related Publications →

Scheduling & Routing

From job-shop scheduling to multi-depot vehicle routing with time windows, we develop scalable, high-performance algorithms that meet the demands of real-world logistics and manufacturing systems.

We focus on dynamic, data-driven approaches that adapt to changing conditions in real-time.
